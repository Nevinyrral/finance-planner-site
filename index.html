Finance planner

A personal budgeting tool with one user: the person who built it.

Finance planner runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a service, it has no accounts, and it is not offered to anyone else. This page exists because Google asks every app that signs in with a Google account to say what it does.

What it does

The owner's banks send an email each time a card is used or money moves. Finance planner reads those alert emails from the owner's own Gmail account and lists the movements in a private dashboard on the same computer, next to the owner's budget.

What it asks Google for

One permission: to read email in the owner's Gmail account (gmail.readonly). It looks only for messages from the bank addresses its owner has listed, and for notes the owner sends to their own address to pass on notifications from their banks' apps. It cannot send, delete or change any email.

Privacy

What the tool reads is kept on the owner's computer. The details, including the short messages it can send to its owner, are in the privacy policy.

Finance planner is a private, non-commercial project.
